## Locked composition

- One principal star above three equal council stars.
- Principal center `(12, 8)`, radius `4`, on a `24 × 24` grid.
- Council centers `(6, 16)`, `(12, 18)`, `(18, 16)`, radius `2`.
- Every star uses the same normalized mother curve.
- Principal-to-council ratio is exactly `2:1`.
- Bilateral symmetry axis is `x = 12`.

Memory instruction: **一颗主星在上，三颗小星成弧在下。**

The principal star represents the shared goal or resolved outcome. It is not a
superior model ruling the three smaller stars.

## Material hierarchy

1. Avatar/app/GitHub first screen: restrained ice-blue glass on a controlled
   midnight field.
2. Light controlled surfaces: solid Charles Blue.
3. Dark controlled surfaces: warm ivory.
4. Monochrome: pure black or white; geometry remains authoritative.

## Identity colors

- Midnight field: `#09142B` to `#172A52`.
- Charles Blue: `#20365F`.
- Warm ivory: `#F4F1EA`.
- Glass body: restrained ice blue through cool periwinkle.

These are identity colors. Functional runtime states use a separate semantic
palette documented in [Visual Design](../docs/VISUAL_DESIGN.md).

## Usage

- Minimum app/favicon size: `16 px` using the same geometry.
- Minimum standalone mark: `24 px`.
- Keep at least one council-star radius around standalone visible bounds.
- Do not add stars, orbit lines, arrows, crowns, faces, text, neon bloom,
  chrome, hard lens flare, or rainbow gradients.
- Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, rearrange, or independently redraw stars.
- Do not use translucent glass directly on white or uncontrolled photography.
