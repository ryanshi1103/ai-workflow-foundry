## Reddit — developer tooling

I use several AI tools, but the hard part is increasingly the workflow between
them: carrying context, deciding permissions, preserving evidence, and knowing
where human approval is required.

FlowFoundry is an early local-first coordination layer built around that
problem. The flagship offline fixture coordinates a release-preparation task
and intentionally stops before release authority. It does not call live models
or publish anything.

## Research community

FlowFoundry is an Alpha experiment in local-first AI coordination: minimum-path
planning, explicit permissions, durable state, evidence, review separated from
approval, and conservative recovery. The public demo uses synthetic providers
so orchestration claims are reproducible without confusing them with model
quality claims.
