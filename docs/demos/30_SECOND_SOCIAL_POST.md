More AI models do not automatically create a better workflow.

FlowFoundry is a Local-first Adaptive AI Team Runtime. Its current coordination
layer starts with a human goal, chooses the minimum sufficient path, preserves
permissions and evidence, recovers from failure, and stops for approval before
consequential action.

The current Alpha has a deterministic offline demo. Mobile control and personal
memory are future work.

**Do not chase every new model. Build a system that coordinates them.**
