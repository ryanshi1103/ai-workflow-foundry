# Codex Contribution Adoption Report

Purpose: apply the same provenance and authority standard used for DeepSeek.
Running later does not make Codex proposals binding, and writing code does not
automatically supersede a completed Meeting or Human decision.

## Material contributions

### 1. Keep FlowFoundry

In `first-real-cross-provider-brand-council-live-1`, Codex independently
selected `KEEP FLOWFOUNDRY` with confidence `0.84`.

What Codex added that DeepSeek did not express as strongly:

- exact fit between workflow/routing and transformation into finished work;
- model neutrality and five-year extensibility beyond current agent fashion;
- explicit repository/CLI/docs/community-memory migration threshold;
- convergence into one validated artifact as a visual idea; and
- disciplined refusal to assert external availability or trademark facts.

Adoption: **BINDING Meeting convergence**. The name is present today.

### 2. Smallest-sufficient tagline and hierarchy

Codex selected `One goal. The smallest sufficient AI team.` in the Brand
Council and again independently in the Visual Identity Council. It contributed
the strongest argument that the tagline converts an implemented routing
principle into a durable, developer-credible product promise.

Codex also supplied the most complete hierarchy preserving:

- mark and wordmark;
- tagline;
- official category;
- one-sentence mechanism;
- quick start;
- compact workflow proof; and
- local/adaptive/execution/validation proof.

Adoption: tagline **BINDING**; hierarchy **ADOPTED**. Both were weakened in
later Codex-led productization and are restored in this reconciliation.

### 3. SYNTHESIS topology

Codex contributed the clearest `Converge to Done` construction: possible paths
resolve through one junction into a visually heavier finished block. It also
identified merge/export and over-technical risks during prototype work.

Adoption: **BINDING historical convergence**, prototyped, then rejected by
Human visual judgment and **validly SUPERSEDED** by the Council Mark. The
contribution remains valuable product-history evidence but is not reinstalled.

### 4. Targeted Round 2 revision

Codex's Round 1 Chinese headline was:

> 一个目标，刚好够用的AI小队

After the conflict pack compared it with DeepSeek's headline, Codex changed its
position to:

> 你定目标，AI组队实现

Codex explicitly recorded `position_changed: true`, `action: revise`,
`resolved: true`, and no remaining dissent. Its Round 2 reasoning separated the
short three-second headline from the ten-second smallest-sufficient explanation.

Adoption: **BINDING shared convergence**. Codex's value here was not originating
the winning copy; it was performing an evidence-based revision that made the
multi-provider Meeting genuinely convergent.

### 5. Runtime implementation and self-bootstrap

Codex authored or integrated major runtime mechanisms reflected by current
source/tests:

- deterministic task profiling and minimum-path modes;
- bounded Meeting state machine;
- cancellation/process identity;
- durable recovery;
- provider/workspace preflight;
- immutable-base writer isolation;
- permission/tool-policy boundaries; and
- truthful usage/cost states.

In `self-bootstrap-1`, Codex independently selected request-metrics candidate
`C1`, emphasizing unchanged prompt semantics, aggregate-only instrumentation,
and deterministic validation. DeepSeek independently confirmed the choice with
the broader cost-evidence chain.

Adoption: **ADOPTED cross-provider decision**, integrated by `f1606f8`.

### 6. Productization, release, and future hierarchy

Later Codex work materially improved:

- current/designed/future maturity separation;
- the GitHub Release Assistant as an honest offline flagship;
- release, privacy, packaging, and no-publication gates;
- contributor onboarding and documentation navigation;
- Personal AI Command Center security boundaries; and
- the long-term Personal AI OS architecture without claiming it as shipped.

Adoption: mostly **ADOPTED**, with future layers **ADVISORY**. The newer content
is preserved.

The drift defect was also Codex-associated: the productized hero silently moved
the advisory launch insight into the tagline slot, moved Coordination Layer
into the category slot, and retained an older logo rather than importing the
Human-approved asset. These were not supported supersessions.
