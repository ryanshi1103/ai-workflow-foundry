# DeepSeek Contribution Adoption Report

Purpose: preserve verified DeepSeek value without promoting a provider proposal
above its actual authority.

## Evidence standard

The strongest attribution comes from durable Meeting call/result receipts that
name `deepseek-reviewer` or `deepseek-visual-view` and preserve the structured
contribution. Historical task headings such as “DeepSeek maintenance leader”
are useful context but are not treated as equivalent to a provider-authenticated
Meeting receipt.

## Material contributions

### 1. Keep FlowFoundry

In `first-real-cross-provider-brand-council-live-1`, DeepSeek independently
selected `KEEP FLOWFOUNDRY` with confidence `0.85` and measured usage/cost.

What DeepSeek added that Codex did not express as strongly:

- a bounded comparison against FlowForge, FlowTeam, and Flowcast;
- the industrial “flow + foundry” metaphor as a distinctive market asset;
- developer-infrastructure credibility;
- poster/visual potential; and
- a direct argument that no alternative cleared the migration-cost threshold.

Adoption: **BINDING Meeting convergence**. The name is fully present today.
The literal flame/anvil logo suggestion from the independent contribution was
not adopted; the later Visual Identity Council rejected literal foundry imagery.

### 2. Chinese headline

In Visual Identity Council Round 1, DeepSeek originated:

> 你定目标，AI组队实现

DeepSeek's reasons were materially distinct:

- direct `你` address passes the three-second student-attention test;
- `AI组队` distinguishes a team-forming system from a chatbot;
- `实现` completes the actor → mechanism → result chain; and
- avoiding `刚好够用` prevents a “barely adequate” reading.

Codex initially proposed `一个目标，刚好够用的AI小队`. The deterministic
conflict gate correctly opened targeted Round 2. Codex then revised to the
DeepSeek headline, citing the same actor/mechanism/result advantage. Final
convergence records no dissent.

Adoption: **BINDING Meeting convergence**. Current state before this
reconciliation: **lost from the public product surface**. It is restored to the
brand/poster guidance, not forced into the GitHub hero.

This is the clearest case where the second provider materially improved the
result. Without DeepSeek, the final Chinese headline would probably have been
the more technical, weaker action chain proposed by Codex.

### 3. Tagline and visual-system testing

DeepSeek independently selected:

- `One goal. The smallest sufficient AI team.`;
- `SYNTHESIS` over pure abstraction or literal foundry; and
- a restrained, non-purple/blue-cliche visual system with open fonts and
  favicon/avatar/monochrome/sticker/poster tests.

Adoption:

- tagline: **BINDING**, but lost from the candidate hero;
- SYNTHESIS: **BINDING at the time, later validly SUPERSEDED by Human visual
  direction**;
- exact teal/amber/slate palette: **participant proposal, not binding final
  convergence**, later superseded by the Human Council palette;
- production-context and anti-cliche tests: **ADVISORY and still valuable**.

### 4. Cost/request observability

In `self-bootstrap-1`, DeepSeek independently selected candidate `C1`: persist
redacted pre-call request-envelope metrics before changing prompt truncation.
DeepSeek contributed the strongest multi-source evidence chain, distinguished
the diagnostic from cost reduction, and rejected higher-risk alternatives.

Adoption: **ADOPTED cross-provider decision**, integrated by commit `f1606f8`.
The mechanism exists in runtime evidence but is intentionally not a GitHub
first-screen feature.

### 5. Historical architecture, maintenance, and UX work

Project records contain substantial DeepSeek-labeled architecture review,
maintenance, launcher, and product critique. The durable effect is visible in:

- monorepo/component boundaries;
- adaptive `cc` layout decisions;
- numeric shortcut implementation evidence;
- privacy/release containment discipline; and
- repeated independent-review gates.

The provider attribution for these non-Meeting sessions is weaker than the
structured live receipts, so this report does not reclassify every line as a
binding “DeepSeek decision.” Their accepted implementations and Human-approved
requirements are ledgered by authority, not by provider prestige.

## Legitimate later improvements

Later Codex productization improved the DeepSeek-influenced result by:

- separating current Coordination Layer, designed Command Center, and future
  Personal AI OS;
- making fake-provider and release boundaries explicit;
- turning broad identity work into an honest runnable demo and contributor path;
- preserving the market insight about coordinated systems; and
- avoiding overclaiming universal provider/cost capability.

Those improvements are retained. The defect was not that later work was poor;
it was that it occupied binding identity slots without importing the Meeting
decisions.
